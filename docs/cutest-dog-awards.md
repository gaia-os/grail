# Who's the cutest?

There's a \$10,000 prize awarded to the cutest dog! Or at least, the most popular.
Contestants can have their friends and families vote for free once per day, or donate $10 for 10 votes, up to $200 dollars for 200 votes.

Every day, the current standings are released, so you know where your dog stands amongst the others.

There's an interesting strategy when you realize the large donations not only improve your chances, but also deliver information about your ranking and how the others are voting.
